About Me

Most L&D portfolios start with the same sentence: "I design learning experiences." Mine starts somewhere different.

I start with the business problem. What does the organization need people to do differently? What's getting in the way? Only then does learning become the answer. That's how I've approached the work across leadership, onboarding, career development, and AI-enabled learning. I don't design courses. I design performance.

I take ownership seriously. When I lead a program, I see it through end to end, from strategy and stakeholder alignment to execution and quality. I'm comfortable managing multiple workstreams at once, and I'm the kind of person teams can count on when priorities shift or things get ambiguous.

I'm also genuinely interested in how emerging tools, especially AI, are reshaping learning, and I'd rather help shape that shift than watch it happen.

How I approach the work

I start with the business problem, not the content.

For me, the success of a learning experience isn’t measured by asking, “Did they complete the course?” It’s measured by whether they can make a sound decision, and explain why.

Onboarding Experience · Performance Support · Process Standardisation

Transforming New Hire Experience

Challenge

Onboarding information — processes, tools, system access, resources, and points of contact — was scattered across multiple sources and heavily reliant on a buddy. This created inconsistent experiences and delayed new hires' ability to contribute.

Strategy

  • Reduce information overload by prioritising relevant resources
  • Create a single, organised experience instead of scattered sources
  • Provide guidance at the right time through a phased journey
  • Help new hires build confidence in the team, tools, and expectations

My Role

Led the end-to-end design — from identifying the opportunity and defining strategy to building the architecture, experience flow, milestones, and content for the first 90 days.

Impact

A focused, intuitive, and consistent onboarding experience. New hires accessed relevant resources faster, built confidence through a structured path, and the team reduced effort to onboard future hires.

Career Development · AI-Assisted Design · Talent Development

My Career Path

Challenge

Store associates had strong onboarding but no clear way to prepare for their next role. Career readiness varied across teams, and the business needed a scalable framework to standardise development across multiple career paths.

Strategy — Learn → Experience → Assess

Designed a guided development programme built on a three-part framework:

LearnFocused foundational content
ExperienceRealistic scenarios & on-the-job application
AssessApplication-based decisions & capstone

Phase 1 covered three pathways: Store Lead → Store Manager, Coach → Store Manager, and Team Lead → Realty Project Coach.

My Role

Co-designed and co-led the end-to-end learning experience — developing content, activities, and assessments, collaborating with global cross-functional stakeholders and SMEs, providing quality reviews, and partnering with the tech team to launch. Leveraged Generative AI throughout for content creation, editing, and QA.

Impact

Exceeded enrolment goals with 73% overall participation. Established a scalable framework for career development — demonstrating how experiential learning, cross-functional collaboration, and AI-assisted design can deliver enterprise learning at scale.

Leadership Development · Blended Learning · Performance Support

Universal Leadership Library

Challenge

Leadership resources were scattered, heavily reliant on LinkedIn Learning, not mobile-friendly, and not inclusive of emerging leaders — limiting continuous, self-directed growth.

Strategy

Approached the library as an experience, not a collection of lessons. Focused on making leadership development practical, accessible, and immediately useful — through concise content, discussion-based learning, practical tools, and performance support leaders could apply in the flow of work.

The library spans six leadership pillars: Courage, Curiosity, Cognizance, Cultural Intelligence, Commitment, and Collaboration.

My Role

Served as the sole instructional designer for the Financial Acumen section. Partnered with LAs, SMEs, and field experts to design focus groups, peer groups, and self-guided experiences — creating interactive activities, ILT outlines, and end-to-end quality assurance.

Impact

  • +13.79% unique visitors month-over-month (27,846 / 31,687)
  • +14.41% page views month-over-month (37,636 / 43,060)
  • Expanded leadership development from mandatory courses into an always-on ecosystem
Certification · Retail Acumen · Curriculum Design

Omni Retail Excellence Certification

Challenge

Entry-level associates had limited visibility into the broader business. The challenge was to build a scalable certification covering retail operations, strategy, customer behaviour, supply chain, finance, and workforce management — without being application- or role-specific.

Strategy

Created a knowledge-transfer-focused, brand-agnostic learning experience inspired by platforms like Coursera. Instead of teaching a specific job, the program helped learners understand the broader forces that shape retail — across five interconnected areas:

  • Retail Operations
  • Omni-Channel Strategy
  • Supply Chain & Inventory
  • Financial Acumen
  • Workforce Management

My Role

As Content Designer and Acting Learning Architect, translated the vision into a cohesive five-course journey — defined the instructional approach, developed content, created the DLO for Financial Acumen, owned scripting and Elucidat development, peer-reviewed assessments for two courses, and supported uLearn deployment.

Impact

By end of Q2 2024, the course reached 116 associates across nine academies with a 37% completion rate — demonstrating early adoption and providing a foundation to scale across a broader associate audience.
